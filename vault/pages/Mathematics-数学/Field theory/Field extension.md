A **field extension** $L/K$ is a pair of fields $K \subseteq L$ in which the operations of $K$ are those of $L$ restricted to $K$. For example, $\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q}$ are field extensions.

## Degree

$L$ is a vector space over $K$, and its dimension is the **degree** $[L : K]$. An extension of finite degree is a **finite extension**. Degrees multiply along a tower $K \subseteq L \subseteq M$:

$$[M : K] = [M : L] \cdot [L : K].$$

## Generated extensions

$K(S)$ is the smallest subfield of $L$ that contains $K$ and the set $S$. An extension $K(s)$ generated by one element is **simple**, and $s$ is then a **primitive element**.

- $[\mathbb{C} : \mathbb{R}] = 2$, with basis $\{1, i\}$, and $\mathbb{C} = \mathbb{R}(i)$.
- $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}) = \{a + b\sqrt{2} \mid a, b \in \mathbb{Q}\}$ has degree $2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
- $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}) = \{a + b\sqrt{2} + c\sqrt{3} + d\sqrt{6} \mid a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Q}\}$ has degree $4$. It is simple too, since it equals $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3})$; see the [[Primitive element theorem]].

## Building extensions

To give a polynomial $f$ a root, take an [[Irreducible polynomial|irreducible]] factor $P$ of $f$, and form $K[X]/(P)$. Repeating this gives the [[Splitting field]] of $f$. An extension in which every element is a root of a polynomial over $K$ is an [[Algebraic extension]].
